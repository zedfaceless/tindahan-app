Delete your Tindahan account

Burahin ang iyong Tindahan account

Option 1, in the app, right away

  • Open Tindahan and go to Account.
  • Scroll to Privacy and your account and tap Delete my account.
  • Type DELETE and tap Delete my account forever.

Your account and data are deleted at once.

Option 2, by email, without the app

Send an email to zedfaces.fernandez@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Tindahan account.

  • Send it from the email address you registered with, so we know the account is yours.
  • Include your Tindahan username.

We will confirm and delete your account within 7 days, and reply to tell you when it is done.

What is deleted. Your account, full name, username, market name, and login. All of your money records, schedules, premium payment requests, income statement requests, help and support tickets with their messages and screenshots, and your app usage events.

What is kept. Nothing is kept on purpose. Deleted data may remain in our providers' backup copies for no longer than 30 days before it is removed. Statements you downloaded or shared yourself are on your own phone or with the people you sent them to, and we cannot delete those.

Deleting cannot be undone. Download any statement you need before you delete.
