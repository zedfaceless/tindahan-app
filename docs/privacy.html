Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Maikling buod. Ang Tindahan ay nag iipon lamang ng impormasyong kailangan para gumana ang app, ang iyong account, ang mga record ng pera na ikaw mismo ang naglalagay, ang iyong mga schedule, at ang iyong mga mensahe sa help and support. Hindi namin ibinebenta ang iyong impormasyon at walang ads. Ikaw ang may ari ng iyong mga record. Maaari mong burahin ang iyong account at lahat ng data nito anumang oras sa loob ng app.

In short. Tindahan only keeps what it needs to work, your account, the money records you enter yourself, your schedules, and your help and support messages. We never sell your information and there are no ads. Your records are yours. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Tindahan is a money tracking app for market vendors in the Philippines. It is made and run by Melchizedek Fernandez, who is responsible for your information under this policy. You can reach us at zedfaces.fernandez@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, full name, username, and market name. Your PIN is protected by our login provider and stored only in a scrambled form that nobody can read, not even us.
  • Your money records. The amounts, descriptions, and dates you enter, and whether each one is business or personal money.
  • Your schedules. The bills and payments you set up, their amounts, due dates, and reminder times.
  • Premium payment requests. In the version installed directly from us, when you pay for premium by GCash you send us the plan, the amount, and the GCash reference number so we can confirm your payment. We do not receive or keep your GCash login, PIN, or card details. The Google Play version of Tindahan does not take payments in the app.
  • Income statement requests. The dates you ask a statement for and whether it was approved. The statement file itself is made on your phone and is only shared if you share it.
  • Help and support tickets. The messages you send us and any screenshots you choose to attach.
  • App usage. Simple events that show how the app is used, for example that the app was opened, so we can improve it. Only the app owner can see these.

Some settings stay only on your phone and are never sent to us, such as your reminder choices, sound setting, and light or dark appearance.

What we do not collect

Tindahan does not access your location, contacts, camera, or microphone. It does not use advertising, ad tracking, or advertising identifiers. When you attach a screenshot, you pick it yourself with your phone's own photo picker, and the app only receives the picture you choose.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and keep your records safe and in sync across your devices.
  • To send the reminders you set up. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • To confirm premium payments and statement requests.
  • To answer your help and support tickets.
  • To keep the service secure and to improve the app.

Who can see your information

  • You. You can see and change your own records at any time.
  • The Tindahan owner and admins. They can see account details and records to give support, approve payments and statements, and keep the service safe. Other vendors can never see your information.
  • Service providers that run Tindahan for us. Supabase hosts our database, file storage, and login, on servers in Singapore. Expo builds and delivers the app. Google delivers the app through Google Play. They may only use your information to provide these services.
  • People you choose. If you share an income statement with a lender or anyone else, they see what is in that statement.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share it if the law requires it, for example a valid court order.

How we protect it

Your information travels between the app and our servers in encrypted form. Our database rules make sure each vendor can only reach their own data. Screenshots are kept in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account and all of the data listed above. Deleted data may remain in our providers' backup copies for a short time, no longer than 30 days, before it is removed.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Account, then Privacy and your account, then Delete my account. You can also ask us to delete it by following the steps on our account deletion page.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, Republic Act No. 10173, you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access your data, to correct it, to have it erased or blocked, to object to its processing, to get a copy of it, and to be paid damages if it is misused. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph. To use any of these rights, email us.

Age

Tindahan is for people who are 18 or older. It is not made for children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app when the change is important.
